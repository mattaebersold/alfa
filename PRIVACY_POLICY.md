# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: May 12, 2026**

## Overview

Open Road Society ("we," "us," or "our") operates the Open Road Society mobile application and website (the "Service"). This policy explains how we handle information you provide when using our Service.

## Information We Collect

When you create an account or use the Service, we may collect:

- **Account information**: username, email address, and password
- **Profile information**: profile photo, bio, and other details you choose to add
- **Content you submit**: posts, photos, car listings, modifications, and other user-generated content
- **Usage data**: pages viewed, features used, and interactions within the app (for basic app functionality)

## How We Store Your Data

Your data is stored securely using:

- **MongoDB** for structured data (account details, posts, car records, etc.)
- **Amazon S3** for media files (photos and images you upload)

## How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide solely to:

- Operate and maintain the Service
- Display your content to other users as intended by the Service
- Authenticate your account and keep it secure
- Respond to your support requests

## What We Do Not Do

We do **not**:

- Sell, rent, or share your personal information with advertisers or third-party marketing companies
- Use your data for targeted advertising
- Claim ownership of any content you upload — your content remains yours

## Content You Share

Content you post (photos, car builds, posts, etc.) is visible to other users of the Service as you intend when you post it. We do not claim ownership of your content. You may delete your content at any time.

## Data Retention

We retain your data as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, your data will be removed from our systems within a reasonable timeframe.

## Third-Party Services

We use Amazon Web Services (AWS) for cloud storage. AWS has its own privacy practices, which you can review at [aws.amazon.com/privacy](https://aws.amazon.com/privacy).

We do not integrate third-party advertising networks or analytics beyond what is required to operate the Service.

## Children's Privacy

The Service is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will notify users of significant changes by updating the "Last Updated" date above.
